---- 2. Where to aim: a one-copy ablation of every shipped card (progress/old-edges/ablation.txt) ----
Every shipped card cut by one copy (39-card deck), all six edges, both modes, ring seeds. Any cut makes a shorter
deck, which draws its good cards more often (session 57), so the table also shows each card against its
faction's average cut. 76 of 89 cards ran; 13 Null cuts are not legal decks (Null sits on the 10/40 removal cap,
so cutting a non-removal card puts it over 25%), so only Null's removal cards were ablated.
Findings that decided the candidates:
  a. Aegis's cards carry it about EQUALLY against Null and against Cortex. Against the average cut, Prime Aegis
     is +7.3 for Null (real) and -7.0 for Aegis vs Cortex; the rest pair the same way within noise (Barrier
     Drone -2.2/+1.7, Static Ward Beacon -1.9/+1.7, Reinforced Bastion +1.8/-2.0 ...). So an Aegis trim moves
     Aegis > Cortex (already OUT low in the gate, 49.3%) along with Null > Aegis. That is why report 031's
     Plating Module trim and report 032's Aegis lists never came out clean. No Aegis-side candidate this time
     (one, NA6, as a check: Aegis Warden without its full heal moves nothing).
  b. Null Pointer (destroy target enemy directive) is Null's dead card: Null does +1.4 better vs Aegis and
     about the same vs Swarm without it (Aegis runs 2 directives, Swarm none); the shipped agent casts it 0.15
     a game vs Aegis, 0.00 vs Swarm (progress/old-edges/mode-use.txt).
  c. Force Quit is Cortex's dead card vs Swarm the same way (Swarm runs no directives): cast 0.00 a game.
So the lead candidates keep each dead card's printed answer and add a second mode ("choose one", the engine's
modal mechanic, session 40) that is live in exactly the matchup the edge needs.

---- 6. Verdict per edge (owner's rule: "if no candidate fixes an edge cleanly, without pushing another edge out
or making a faction dominant, both edges stay the accepted-out edges") ----
Null > Aegis: NOT fixed. The best candidate (NA12) closes about half the real-match gap (45.9 -> 50.3 on shipped
  decks; +3.2 to +4.1 at the pack levels), reaches the band in the gate (56.3; 54.2-57.1 at the pack levels), and
  is clean everywhere else (Swarm > Null -1.0, Null ~ Cortex stays even at 50-54, no faction mean leaves 46.8-53.1)
  but does nothing from a full collection. It is a real partial fix, not a fix. The cause on file stands: in the
  real-match mode Aegis gets ~+10 from its signature card, and no Null card answers that in full.
Cortex > Swarm: FIXED in the real-match mode by CS7, with a cost. 53.0 -> 55.0 on shipped decks, 56.2-56.8 at
  37/17 packs and 56.4 at 3 (inside 55-60; the intervals still touch 55, so UNRESOLVED, no longer OUT); Aegis > Cortex and Null ~ Cortex hold. But the gate overshoots: 61.3 on
  shipped decks, 61.2-63.3 at the pack levels (band top 60). The ring check would pass it as built (UNRESOLVED,
  not OUT, on the ring's seeds), but the edge would sit above its band whenever no signature cards are in play.
Both together: the two lifts add up on their own edges, and Null ~ Cortex climbs to 55.3 (real, shipped decks,
  pooled) / 51-52 at the pack levels. Shipping both needs that watched.
