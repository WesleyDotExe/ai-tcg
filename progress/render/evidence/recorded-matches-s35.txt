# Victory boasts, slice 3: replays read the boast from the match record

Each scenario runs through an in-memory economy store (fixed clock and seeds): starter collections
(with the four free starter boasts), an agent each, starter decks; season items earned in real
matches and bought; loadouts equipped; then the replayed match started with startMatch, captured
move by move and recorded with each side's final health. The replay plays the record's boast.

## recorded-boot-sequence (Cortex vs Aegis, seed 2)
a bought season boast in the main slot: the Cortex winner plays Boot Sequence, not its faction's Data Cascade

earning: 37 real matches over 4 days (Kestrel Nine won 24, Velvet Static 13)
bought: Kestrel Nine buys Boot Sequence; credits left 1
p1 Kestrel Nine (Cortex): boast=Boot Sequence  closeWinBoast=-
p2 Velvet Static (Aegis): boast=Shield Slam  closeWinBoast=-
match: 97 moves over 13 turns; Kestrel Nine wins on 11 health
record recorded-boot-sequence: boast Boot Sequence (slot boast, p1, winner on 11)
wrote render/output/recorded-boot-sequence.html
