From a full collection. Build: 60 one-copy changes per faction, 100 games per shipped opponent each; confirm on 500 per opponent (fresh seeds from 1000001).
Cross: every style pilots every style's deck, 500 games per shipped opponent (2000 per cell), seeds from 3000001, unseen by any build.
Search and confirm both play the built deck in the style (the --search own experiment).
The four shipped opponents always play Calculator. Win rates count draws as not won.
