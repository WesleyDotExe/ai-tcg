AI-TCG play-style ring 001 (session 52, roadmap 2b part 2)

Six play styles, each a preset of the LookaheadAgent's weights (engine/src/agents/playStyles.ts).
Calculator is today's agent exactly (a test pins it move for move). Produced by:
  cd engine && npx tsx src/dev/playStyleRing.ts run --seeds 300 --first 5001 --shard i/4 --out f-i.json   (i = 0..3)
  npx tsx src/dev/playStyleRing.ts report f-?.json
Re-running gives the same numbers (checked: the shipped presets reproduce the tuning run byte for byte).

How the presets were found (all on seeds 1-100 or fewer, then confirmed on fresh seeds 5001-5300):
  First draft (30 seeds): Calculator +3.1, Rusher +5.1, Controller -6.3, Tempo +5.7, Fortress +0.9, Gambler -8.6
    points against the field. Bold styles were unfair: holding up responses, attacking units over
    the opponent's health and a wide random pick cost Controller and Gambler 6-9 points.
  One weight at a time against Calculator (100 seeds, 3,200 games each): spending faster, a higher
    face weight, valuing bodies more and a little reckless trading are close to free (within 1
    point); a higher trade weight (-5.6), a lower face weight (-3.8), a wide Gambler margin (1.5:
    -8.5) and holding energy for an Instant (-2.7) cost the most. Heal/buff and card-draw weights
    barely change a decision (under 1.5% of decisions), so Fortress got a new weight, toughness.
  Four rounds of adjustment (30-150 seeds) brought every style inside 2 points; one confirm at
    seeds 1001-1300 failed Controller (-2.1), its reserve was lowered 1.0 -> 0.6 and its removal
    1.4 -> 1.3, and the result below is the confirm on seeds no tuning run had used.

The "off Calc" column is the share of decisions with more than one legal move where the style
picked a move Calculator would not have (not among Calculator's best in the same position).
