Balance report 035: Null's 4 added cards (session 59, the pre-launch test-only balance session, job 1)
=====================================================================================================
Owner 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-29: every starter deck is the same size, so Null's starter and shipped list grow
from 36 to 40. The 4 cards are chosen here, measured on the ring check (both modes) and on the built-deck ring
at every collection level, and brought to an owner check-in. TEST ONLY: no card, deck or rule changed. Every
candidate below was played in a throwaway copy of the repo; nothing in engine/src/data changed.

Tools: engine/src/dev/nullFortySearch.ts (search, heldout, pool, built) and progress/null-forty/run-candidate.sh
(the built-deck ring for one candidate). Raw files: progress/null-forty/.

---- 0. What can be added ----
One more copy of a Null card under its copy limit, or a neutral-pool card (at most 10 neutral). Zero-Day cards are
left out: they are secret cards, never in a starter (today's builder fills Null's 40 with Patient Zero from a full
collection; a starter can't do that). At 37-39 cards, 18 adds are legal: Null already runs 9 removal/debuff copies,
and a 10th is over the 25% cap until the deck reaches 40 (10/40 = 25%). So at most ONE of the 4 can be removal
(Segfault Handler, Null Pointer, Bit Rot, Kernel Panic, ...), and with it Null sits exactly at the removal cap.

---- 1. The search (search seeds 300001-301500, 1,500 per edge per mode; progress/null-forty/search.txt) ----
Every single add made Null slightly weaker on these seeds (mean 46.0 -> 43.9-46.2), the short-deck effect
session 57 measured. Greedy picks (best ring objective with a no-sandbagging guard) were:
  A (greedy line):     Rollback Point, Fork Bomb, Rollback Point, Segfault Handler
  B (runner-up line):  Scrap Recycler, Rollback Point, Dangling Pointer, Rollback Point
Honest limit: the steps' differences are mostly noise (about 1.3 points per edge on 1,500 games; one step's best
39-card list scored better than its 38-card parent and the next step's every option scored worse). So the search
only proposes. Two Null-only sets were added by hand for comparison, since a starter of faction cards may matter
to the owner:
  C (Null bodies only):    Fork Bomb, Zombie Process, Dangling Pointer, Corrupted Process
  D (Null, one removal):   Fork Bomb, Zombie Process, Dangling Pointer, Segfault Handler

Reading: with the shipped decks, 4 more cards cost Null nothing measurable (mean -0 to +1.3). The one real move is
Swarm > Null: with 40 cards Null does better against Swarm (B, C, D move it down 2.7-3.6 points, out of noise);

Ring check verdicts on the ring's own seeds (heldout.txt): every set turns gate Swarm > Null from IN BAND (57.1) to

for every candidate until the baseline is re-recorded with the owner's OK. D also turns real Swarm > Null from
UNRESOLVED to OUT (52.6). Any 40 does this; it is the price of the same-size rule, not of one card.

Reading:
- Every candidate beats today's builder fill for Null at every level (real-match mode +1.9 to +6.6 points). The rating fill
  was the problem session 58 found, and choosing the 4 cards fixes most of it.
- A brings the whole field back to about where session 54's short decks had it (real spread 5.9 vs 6.0), with
  Null within 4.2 points of 50 everywhere in real mode, and it lifts Null > Aegis with built decks the most
  (47.8-49.1 real at full/37/17, where today's is 37.2-45.3).
- D gives Null the highest pack-level means, but at full collection Aegis's built deck runs away (60.4 real /
  62.7 gate) and Swarm sinks (46.5-48.9 real): Swarm > Null drops to 51-53, under Swarm's 55-60 band.
- B (A with Scrap Recycler and Dangling Pointer for Fork Bomb and Segfault Handler) is second: close to A in real
  mode, the steadiest Null in gate mode (45.0-46.8), but it takes Swarm > Null under Swarm's band at every level
  (51.9-54.3). The two sets with 2 Rollback Point (A, B) even the field best; both lift Null > Aegis with built decks.
- C (no removal) helps least.
- Honest limits: the "full" column is ONE built deck per faction, so it carries the builder's search noise (Aegis's
  full-collection mean ranges 50.0-60.4 real / 52.2-62.7 gate across these runs with Aegis's own cards unchanged; only the shipped Null
  deck it is searched against differs). The pack columns (8 owners per faction) are the steadier read. A and D
  put Null at the 25% removal cap (10 of 40); B and C don't. No candidate fixes Null > Aegis on the shipped ring check;
  that edge stays job 2.

---- 4. Recommendation (for the owner check-in) ----
Add A: +1 Fork Bomb (2 -> 3), +1 Segfault Handler (1 -> 2), +2 Rollback Point (a neutral 3-energy "restore 5
health" Command; Cortex's starter already runs one). It changes the shipped ring the least (no edge moves by a real
amount) and evens out the built-deck ring the most. Alternative if the owner wants a Null-only starter: D (+1 Fork
Bomb, +1 Zombie Process, +1 Dangling Pointer, +1 Segfault Handler), which makes Null stronger with built decks but
leaves the field less even (Aegis high at full collection, Swarm low). Either way the ring check needs its
baselines re-recorded with the owner's OK, since gate Swarm > Null leaves IN BAND for every 40.
