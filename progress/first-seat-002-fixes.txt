# The four first-seat fixes, measured (session 68)

Owner 2026-11-02: measure (a)-(d) on the shipped-deck mirrors and the ladder's seat split, the leaders through
the ring check and the built-deck ring, test only. No rule changed: each variant is a `rules` option the
measuring instruments pass to runMatch (engine/src/state/types.ts MatchRules); no match the game plays sets it.
The second seat is always p2. Extras (doses and pairs) are marked; the four asked are (a)-(d).
50% is a fair seat. The aim is the variant whose first-seat share is closest to 50% without moving the ring.
