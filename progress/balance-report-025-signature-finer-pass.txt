AI-TCG balance report 025: the finer tuner pass, and the reworked Guardian and Bulwark (session 34)
Owner 2026-10-15, option A: Guardian becomes Barrier only and gets one more tuner pass; Bulwark becomes
'takes the first attack aimed at one of your units (never you)' and stays off the generator until it
passes; the three near misses (Smite, Energy surge, Sweep) get a finer tuner pass.

The test (unchanged): swap the candidate in for one faction's plain 3/6 stand-in; it passes when every one of
the twelve edges moves by 2.0 points or less (paired change, same seeds). Only a 2,500-seed measurement counts.

Commands (from engine/), all seeded, so each reproduces its lines exactly:
  grid:    npx tsx src/dev/signatureFairness.ts grid --abilities smite,energy-surge,sweep,guardian,bulwark --seeds 1000 --shard i/4 --out grid-i.jsonl
  confirm: npx tsx src/dev/signatureFairness.ts confirm --in grid-0..3.jsonl --top 5 --seeds 2500 --shard i/4 --out grid-confirm-i.jsonl
  levers:  npx tsx src/dev/signatureFairness.ts levers --ability guardian|bulwark --coarse 300 --seeds 500 --confirm 2500 --out levers-s34-K.jsonl

What 'finer' means here. Session 33's tuner screened table lines at 300 seeds and walked greedily from the 3 best
configs at 500, so a line next to a noisy screen was never measured. This pass measures EVERY size of each option at
EVERY cost-5 body with attack 2-4 and health 3-9 (21 bodies), all at 1,000 seeds, then re-measures the 5 best lines
of each option at 2,500. Nothing depends on which line happened to screen well.
