FIRST: A FIX TO SESSION 43'S DRAW.
  Session 43's real-match stand-in drew each side's line as (seed * 7 + seat * 3) % 14. Because 7 * seed mod 14
  is only ever 0 or 7, that reached 2 of the 14 approved lines per seat (lines 0 and 7 for the first-named side,
  3 and 10 for the other), not all 14 as its check-in said (and the owner's smaller call 1 accepted). The new
  ring test caught it. The draw is now edgeMeasure.approvedSignatureFor: over each block of 28 seeds every line
  is drawn twice per seat, once going first and once going second, and the two seats never draw the same line
  on a seed. The test pins all three properties over seeds 1-2,500.
  What it changed (2,500 seeds per edge; 4 lines -> 14 lines):
    Null > Aegis    45.2% -> 45.4%   (the headline stands)
    Aegis > Cortex  54.2% -> 56.1%
    Cortex > Swarm  52.9% -> 53.4%   (OUT -> UNRESOLVED)
    Swarm > Null    56.3% -> 55.8%
  The per-faction gains from section 2b of balance-report-030 change more (below): with all 14 lines a signature
  card is worth +9.9 points to Aegis on average and +0.9 to +3.1 to the others; the "twice as much" was closer to
  three to ten times as much.

== Reading ==

1. Why a signature card is worth most to Aegis. Three measurements, one negative:
   a. SPARE ENERGY (measured, and partly confirmed by an ablation). On late turns (5+ max energy) without a
      signature card, Aegis ends its turn with 2.93 energy unspent; the others leave 1.84-2.17. Aegis ends 47.8%
      of those turns with 3+ unspent (others 27.6-35.2%) and 12.5% with an empty hand. Aegis's deck is built
      around 1-3 cost cards; once its hand is spent it has nothing to do with 5+ energy. A signature card is a
      5-cost play that is always there, so for Aegis it is close to free: it puts 1.72 energy a late turn to use,
      against 1.13-1.28 for the others. Section 5 checks the cause: a heavier Aegis deck (3 Cadets swapped for
      3 vanilla 5-drops) leaves ~0.5 less unspent and cuts Aegis's gain by about 3 points on each edge
      (+10.2 -> +6.9, +12.8 -> +9.6, +6.7 -> +4.2). So spare energy is about a third of the gap, not all of it.
   b. THE BODY BECOMES AEGIS'S ATTACKER (measured, correlational). Aegis's signature card makes the most attacks
      (1.60-2.11 a game) and deals the most face damage (3.59-5.53 a game; Cortex 3.36-4.16, Swarm 1.97-2.86,
      Null 1.68-3.51), and draws the most attacks aimed at it (0.26-0.36 a game; others 0.01-0.14). Aegis's own
      deck is walls and low attack; a 3-attack body looks like the closer Aegis otherwise lacks. Not tested by an ablation this session.
   c. NOT TOOLS. Aegis's signature card carries the most tools (0.38-0.51 a game; Null 0.18-0.44, Cortex
      0.11-0.17, Swarm 0.04-0.08), but a rule that no signature card can carry tools (T1) moves Null > Aegis only
      +1.4 (within noise). Tool stacking is not what drives it.

2. Proposed fixes for Null > Aegis (real-match mode, 45.4%, 9.6 under 55). None of six trials reaches the band:
     T1 no tools on a signature card (rule)      Null > Aegis +1.4 (noise); nothing else moves
     T2 Kernel Panic reach 4 -> 5                 +0.0 everywhere (it evolves in ~11% of Null's games vs Aegis)
     T3 Kernel Panic reach 4 -> 6                 +0.4 (noise)
     T4 Plating Module +0/+3 -> +0/+2             Null > Aegis +1.5 (noise), Aegis > Cortex -1.8 real / -2.7 gate
     T5 Null Singularity cost 7 -> 6              Null > Aegis +3.8 real / +3.3 gate (49.2% / 53.9%), Null ~ Cortex
                                                  +3.3 (stays IN), but Swarm > Null -3.3 real (52.4%, OUT) / -3.9 gate
     T6 every signature card costs 6 (rule)      Null > Aegis +2.8 (borderline), Aegis > Cortex -3.3 (52.8%, OUT)
   The best single change, T5, closes 3.8 of the 9.6 points and pushes Swarm > Null out of band.

3. Recommendation (at the check-in): no card change now. Keep Null > Aegis as the real-match check's one
   accepted-OUT edge, and design the fix into the next set where it fits the cause: give Aegis a real late-game
   play so its late turns aren't empty (section 5 says this shrinks the signature gain), and give Null an answer
   to a durable attacker. T5 is the one-card partial fix if the owner wants movement now.

4. Limits: 2,500 seeds per cell. Section 5's heavier deck is a measuring tool (vanilla units, outside the lint),
   and it also makes Aegis weaker in the gate (-5 to -7 points), so it shows the direction of the cause, not its
   exact size. The approved draw is even over the 14 lines; real owners pick 1 of 3. The "body becomes the
   attacker" reading is correlational.
