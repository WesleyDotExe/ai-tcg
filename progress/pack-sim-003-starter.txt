AI-TCG starter guarantee simulation (session 46): every new owner sees a hit across the 3 starter packs
Command (from economy/): npx tsx src/dev/starterSimulation.ts. Live table S0-odds-2; 3 starter packs per owner.
"Hit" = Full Art or better: fullArt, glitch, altArt, gold, secret, zeroDay, prototype. Base hit rate of a shine slot: 18.4%.
200,000 simulated new owners per method, the same seeds for every method (owner i opens seeds 3i+1..3i+3).
Methods: none = the store before session 46; A = BUILT: the last starter pack's shine slot is a hit when the first two had none;
B = for comparison only, not built: if none of the 3 has a hit, one of the 3 picked at random gets its shine slot upgraded.

   Hit pity counts from the owner's history, so a starter hit near the end of the starters restarts the 10-pack count
   later. The starters are 3 packs of 43 here, so the later rows move by hundredths at most: the guarantee is a
   one-off at sign-up, not a change to the odds of any pack an owner buys or earns.

3. Supply: what the guarantee adds to the whole economy

   A adds 0.543 hit copies per new owner (0.555 -> 1.098), the hits of about 2.9 ordinary packs' shine slots.
   Zero-Day cards per 10,000 new owners: 62.0 -> 121.3. Secret illustrations per 10,000 new owners: 443.9 -> 893.7.
   Prototypes: unchanged. The guarantee only touches the shine slot, never the bonus 6th card, so no run is used up faster.
   Mythics: the Rare slot and the Mythic pity timer are untouched; the owners holding a Mythic card rise only because an
   upgraded Full Art, Alt Art or Gold can land on a Mythic (15-30% of those) and a Zero-Day card is a Mythic.

   An owner opening a pack sees the same pack and the same tear in every case: the guarantee is worked out when the pack
   is opened, from the owner's own starter packs, and only the opening's record (`starterHit`) says it applied.
   Under A an owner who knows the rule can tell, after packs 1 and 2 had no hit, that pack 3 will (pack 3: 72.6% hit).
   Under B each of the 3 packs is equally likely to hold the guaranteed hit (36.6% / 36.5% / 36.5%), so packs 1 and 2 give less away;
   it would need all 3 starter packs rolled when the first is opened (or seeds drawn at grant and kept secret), plus a
   separate draw for the upgraded pack (see the note in `starters`), a bigger change to the store for the same total.
   A needs neither: the store knows the guarantee is due before it draws pack 3's seed, as with pity, so the upgraded
   finish comes out in the table's own proportions (section 1).
