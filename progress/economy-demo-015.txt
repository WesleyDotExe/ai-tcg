AI-TCG economy demo 015: play styles in the store (schema v13)

1. The presets the owner app lists:
   Calculator  Weighs every move on the board as it stands and takes the best. The balanced default.
   Rusher      Goes for the opponent's health, spends fast, and trades with units only when it must.
   Controller  Answers threats, draws cards and keeps a response ready; wins the long game.
   Tempo       Uses its energy every turn to put the wider board down first.
   Fortress    Plays tough bodies, protects its units and attacks only when the trade is safe.
   Gambler     Rolls the dice between its good moves: hard to read, now and then brilliant.
