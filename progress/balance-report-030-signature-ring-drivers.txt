  ringRegression.ts --signature puts each faction's SET MYTHIC (Prime Aegis, Quorum Arbiter, Hive Mother,
  Null Singularity) in the signature zone. It was recorded in session 31 as a "what if" (option A,
  2026-10-11: the gate stays as it is; these numbers stay as a second baseline only). A real match does
  something else: the store's match setup (economy/src/store.ts, `deck.spec.signature = signature.card`)

== Reading ==

1. In the mythic mode, the 71.5% is Prime Aegis being always available. Moving Prime Aegis alone to the
   zone is worth +33.0 points to Aegis against Cortex (+33.0 again with Quorum Arbiter already there);
   Quorum Arbiter in the zone only gives Cortex 10.8 back. Prime Aegis comes down on Aegis's 6th turn
   in 95% of games and dies in 1.4% of them: Cortex has nothing that removes a 4/8 that makes itself 5/9
   and shrinks every Cortex attacker by 1. Aegis wins 75.0% of the games it sticks, 20.1% of the rest.
   It is not the replay rule (returns 0.01 a game), and not how the agent plays it (it plays it on curve
   every time it can). It is guaranteed access to a card balanced as a 1-in-4 draw (the gate plays it in
   29% of games, winning 80.3% of those). Session 31 said the same; the agent fix did not change it.

2. The other mythic-mode edges far out of band, and what drives each (section 2a):
     Null > Aegis    30.5% (24.5 under)  Prime Aegis to the zone -29.8; Null Singularity only +10.8 (cost 7,
                                          played in 48% of games: games end first)
     Cortex > Swarm  43.9% (11.1 under)  Hive Mother to the zone -19.6 for Cortex; Quorum Arbiter only +4.4
     Swarm > Null    64.8% (4.8 over)    Hive Mother +13.8; Null Singularity -5.2
     Aegis ~ Swarm   56.1% (1.1 over)    Prime Aegis +23.0 against Hive Mother -19.4
   So the mythic mode ranks the four Mythics in the zone: Prime Aegis (~+23 to +33) > Hive Mother (~+14 to
   +20) > Null Singularity (~+5 to +11) ~ Quorum Arbiter (~+4 to +11). Its edges measure that ranking,
   not the factions.

3. In the mode real matches use, Aegis > Cortex is close to its band. With the approved lines it is 54.2%
   (UNRESOLVED, 0.8 under 55); with the plain stand-in 55.2%. A signature card on each side actually
   lifts the gate's OUT edge (49.3%) toward the band. The 71.5% does not describe a real match.
