AI-TCG balance report 024: the Neutral signature menu with the tuner's new levers (session 33, 2026-10-14)
======================================================================================================================

The test is unchanged from report 023 (owner 2026-10-11, option A): every faction plays its shipped deck with the
same plain Neutral 5-cost 3/6 signature card; a candidate replaces ONE faction's plain card, and passes when each of
that faction's three edges moves 2.0 points or less, for all four factions (12 edges). Read on the paired point
estimate (owner 2026-10-13, call 1: the measured change on the same games, not the whole interval).

What is new (owner 2026-10-13, option A):
  - The size lever. Every option except Guardian and Bulwark has smaller and/or larger sizes in the menu data
    (engine/src/data/signatureMenu.ts, MenuOption.sizes): the same mechanic with a number turned up or down, a
    narrower target, or an 'only while' condition. Guardian and Bulwark have no size between all and nothing.
  - The cost-tier lever. Each size is tried at cost 5, at cost 6 with each keyword it doesn't print (Rush, Guard,
    Barrier), and at cost 7 with each other aura (Commander's aura, Shield wall, Dampen, Tax).
  - Attack stays at 2 or more everywhere (the walk no longer goes below 2).
  - Bulwark now stands aside while a Guard unit protects its side (owner 2026-10-13, call 3), measured here.

Method, per option (signatureFairness.ts tuneWithLevers):
  1. every table stat line of every size x tier x extra, at 300 seeds per edge (the coarse screen);
  2. from the 3 configs with the best table line, the stat walk (attack 2-6, health 1-9) at 500 seeds;
  3. the 3 best lines over all of it re-measured at 2,500 seeds. Only a 2,500-seed result counts as a pass.
Command (from engine/): npx tsx src/dev/signatureFairness.ts levers --shard i/4 --coarse 300 --seeds 500 --confirm 2500 \
  --out ../progress/signature-fairness/levers-i.jsonl     (i = 0..3, in parallel; everything is seeded)

5. What the numbers say
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The size lever is the one that works. All four new passes are a smaller size at cost 5 on or next to the
    table: Rally +0/+1 at 3/6, Restore on one random unit at 3/6, Supply line only from a hand of 2 or fewer
    at 3/6, Inspire +0/+1 at 3/4 or 3/5. Recycle and Tax still pass as before (Recycle now also at 1 card).
  - The cost lever rarely helps. Cost 6 still plays above the reference for almost every option; cost 7 lands
    near it in net terms, but only with bodies far off the table (Guardian 3/2, Bulwark 3/4), and its
    second aura splits the factions: Cortex and Swarm up 3-6, Aegis down 2-6.
  - Three options are close (worst edge 2.4-2.6): Smite at 1 damage, Energy surge as written, Sweep on 2
    random units. Each misses on one edge, and a 1-health step is the finest lever left, so another tuner
    pass at finer resolution (more seeds per step, more starting points) is worth a run before reworking them.
  - Commander's aura, Hunter, Shield wall, Suppress and Dampen fail at every size and tier (3.2-5.2). Hunter is
    simply too strong even at its smallest size (net +2.7, every edge up). The other four show report 023's
    pattern: at a net-even line the factions still split, Aegis down 1.6-3.6 points on some edge while another
    faction gains 3.2-4.8 on one of its own, so the 12 edges can't all sit inside 2 at once.
  - Guardian and Bulwark fail by the most, as you expected. Guard is the problem, not Barrier: Barrier alone at
    3/4 misses by 0.1 (2.1), Guard alone at 3/3 is still 8.1 off. Every attack-pulling version of Bulwark
    fails too (3.9-9.1), the 'only attacks aimed at your units' version least.
