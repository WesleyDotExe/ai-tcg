AI-TCG pack simulation (session 36)
Set S0 "Season Zero": 96 cards. Odds table S0-odds-1 (economy/src/config.ts PACK_ODDS).
Command (from economy/): npx tsx src/dev/packSimulation.ts. Every pack is rollPack(seed, ...), the function openPack uses.

1. Published rates against 400,000 simulated packs (pity off, so these are the base odds)

   Shine slot Full Art               10%            10.008%

   Shine slot Gold                   ~1 in 150      0.682%

   Prototype bonus 6th card          1 in 1,000     0.099%

   Shine slot rarity (PROPOSED weights 60 / 25 / 12 / 3; an Alt Art roll draws a Rare or Mythic):
   Common 57.92%, Uncommon 24.35%, Rare 14.16%, Mythic 3.57%
   Any Mythic in a pack (Rare slot or shine slot): about 16.01% of packs.
   Every card, every slot (2,000,395 cards): standard 91.148%, foil 6.029%, fullArt 2.010%, altArt 0.655%, gold 0.137%, prototype 0.020%

2. Pity per owner: 2,000 owners x 120 packs each, timers carried pack to pack as openPack does

   Full Art or better pity (after 10 dry packs) fired in 3.59% of packs; the longest dry run was 10.
   Mythic pity (after 40 dry packs) fired in 0.011% of packs; the longest dry run was 40.
   ("Full Art or better" = fullArt, altArt, gold, prototype. With ~14% of packs naturally Full Art or better,
   10 dry packs in a row is about a 1 in 5 event, so the timer fires often; with ~16% of packs holding a
   Mythic, 40 dry packs is rare, so that timer is a backstop. Both numbers are the owner's.)

   median 130 packs (10th percentile 96, 90th 188); every Mythic: median 45.
