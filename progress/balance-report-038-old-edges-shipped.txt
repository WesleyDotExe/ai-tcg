---- 3. One finding: Null Out never kills an undamaged unit ----
The engine floors max health at 1 (state/queries.ts currentMaxHealth; the same rule already pinned for
Deprecated Routine in nullFaction.test.ts: "decay" kills only together with damage). So Null Out on an
undamaged 3/3 leaves a 0/1, alive. It kills any unit of 4 or less health that has taken any damage. Session 61's
candidate note ("kills a 3-health body") was wrong about this. Its NUMBERS are still right: the candidate was
measured under the same floor, so the shipped card is the one measured. Now pinned by tests
(engine/test/oldEdgesShipped.test.ts). Raised for the owner: the card text "-3/-3" reads as if it kills a
3-health unit.
