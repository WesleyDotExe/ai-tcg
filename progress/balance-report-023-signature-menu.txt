5. What the numbers say
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Attack is the lever, health is not. One attack point moves a candidate ~5 points net; one health point
    moves it a few tenths (a signature card rarely dies: in a 100-game Cortex-vs-Swarm check, a 3/6 died in 3). Integer attack steps are
    coarser than the 2-point limit, so for most options no stat line lands inside it.
  - Net ~0 is not enough: the same option helps the factions unevenly. Paid for with attack, an ability
    usually costs Aegis the most (Aegis gains most from a raw body), so a line that is net-even still
    leaves Aegis 3-6 points down and another faction 3-6 up (Smite 1/6, Commander's aura 1/3, Dampen 1/2).
  - On the table, only the two options that barely change a game pass: Recycle (it only fires when the
    card dies) and Tax. Guardian (Guard + Barrier) is the strongest by far (worst edge +40 to +47 on the table).
  - The 6-cost tier plays far above the plain 5-cost reference (net up to +32); the 7-cost tier lands near
    it (net -0.8 to +8.4), because a 7-cost card arrives late in ~13-turn games.
