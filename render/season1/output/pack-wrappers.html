Booster packs: three wrapper designs

The sealed pack as an object: crimped seals top and bottom, a pillow curve across the face, light on the edges, a highlight that slides as the pack turns, a holographic sheen, a soft reflection and a glow. Move the pointer over the page (or tilt a phone) and the packs follow it; left alone, the light sweeps slowly. With reduced motion it holds still.

Every design carries the AI·TCG wordmark, the set's name as its title ("Set name" until the set is named), "Season 1" and the set code S1-01 small, and the set symbol. The mascots are placeholder art; January's art pass fills the same space.
