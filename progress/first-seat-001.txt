# The first seat's win rate over a large ladder run (session 67)

Eight house owners (two per faction, six play styles), the server's ladder with the house pack policy on;
600 rounds of 4 matches, 7 minutes of clock each (12 Sydney days); 108 deck builds and rebuilds.
Seats are a coin flip, so the first seat's share of decided matches is the first-seat edge.

## Overall

matches 2400; decided 2400; draws 0; cancelled 0; abandoned 0
first seat won 1554 of 2400 = 64.8% (95% interval 62.8-66.7%); two-sided p = 0.0000 against 50%
verdict: OUTSIDE noise: the first seat has a real edge at this sample size
for scale: session 66's demo had 40 of 71 decided (56.3%); at 71 matches the 95% interval is about +/-11.6 points.
