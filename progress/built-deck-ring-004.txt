The built-deck ring (pre-launch gate, owner 2026-10-19): the six ring edges with the deck-building agent's decks on both sides
Lookahead vs Lookahead, Calculator style, ring seeds (2,500 for the win/lose edges, 900 for the even ones, seeds from 1).
Builds: the store's own (chooseDeck's defaults: 40 games per shipped opponent, 24 one-copy changes, 100-game confirm, search seed 1), forced to the owner's starting faction.
Realistic owners: 8 per faction, each the faction's starter collection plus packs from the live roller (economy/src/dev/realisticCollections.ts);
at a pack level each seed seats one owner per side, every owner equally often and on both seats.
Bands: predator 55-60%, prey 40-45%, even 45-55%. Verdicts on the 95% Wilson interval: IN only when it sits inside the band, OUT only when it misses it.
No card, deck or rule changed: this only measures.
