Season 1 · pack opening · storyboardThe new opening: build-up first, light within the limit

The owner's direction (2026-11-22): the pack opening is jarring with the flashing lights. The build-up is shake, tear and sound, not strobe; the reveal and the big pulls stay big, within WCAG 2.3.1 (no more than three flashes in any one second, no saturated red flashing), with a reduced-flash setting. This storyboard is what phase 3 builds. Placeholder shapes only: the wrapper, mascots and cards get real art through the art plan.

The limit (WCAG 2.3.1)No more than three flashes in any second over more than a quarter of a 10° field of view; no saturated red flashing. Checked automatically.
The design's budgetAt most 1 counted flash in any second; big lights at least 1 s apart; a bright light rises over 250 ms or more and falls over 400 ms or more.
Reduced flashEvery light stays under 0.08 (a change has to reach 0.1 to count), so nothing flashes at all; shakes and spins off with reduced motion.
The moment is keptThe size of a pull lives in the build's length, the sound, the shake, the ward and the motes, and in one bloom per hit.

The beats (a Gold pull)

1. The pack arrives

time · about 1.4 s

See
The room dims to the neon night (the grid running to a horizon behind the glass). The sealed pack rises into the middle: a crimped foil wrapper, the set logo along the top crimp, the faction mascot as the hero (placeholder art until the owner approves the art), the set symbol low on the front. A slow sheen crosses the foil once every 4 s.
Hear
A low room tone fades in; a soft whoosh as the pack rises.
Move
The pack rises 40 px and settles; an idle bob of 6 px every 3.4 s.
Reduced flash
The pack fades in in place; no bob; the sheen stays (a sheen's light change is under 0.05).
Season 0
Season 0: the pack rises and bobs with a shader shimmer; the page's first second measured 1-2 flashes over 80-88% of a field of view as the stage and the pack draw in (pack=1..10, worst second at 0.0-0.5 s). The new room fades in over 600 ms, which is under the flash line.

2. Grip

the owner presses and holds the pack (or taps: the same beat, 600 ms) · about 0.9 s

See
The pack is held: it leans toward the finger and trembles, harder the longer it is held. The honest tell from Season 0 stays: when the pack holds a hit (Full Art or better), the top crimp's seam starts to glow in the best card's colour, rising and holding, never pulsing.
Hear
The foil crinkles under the finger; a held tone rises with the tremble.
Move
Tremble: 0.5 px growing to 3 px, 18 times a second (a shake, not a light: it moves the pack, the light stays still).
Reduced flash
No tremble (reduced motion); the seam's glow is capped under the flash line.
Season 0
Season 0 has no grip beat: a tap tears at once. The owner asked for the build-up to be shake, tear and sound, not light.

3. The tear

the owner drags across the top of the pack, any way, a third of its width (a tap or Enter plays it by itself in 600 ms; owner 2026-11-28 point 9) · about 0.9 s

See
The foil rips along the finger's path: a jagged edge, curling scraps thrown off the head of the tear, the inside showing through the cut. The seam's glow becomes light leaking through the cut, one steady rise as the cut lengthens.
Hear
A tearing sound whose pitch and grain follow the finger's speed; the held tone swells.
Move
The strip peels; scraps fly; the pack sways with the pull.
Reduced flash
The strip slides off without scraps; the leak rises under the flash line.
Season 0
Season 0's tear throws sparks for a hit (kept: the honest tell) and ends in the burst below.

4. Open

time (the tear completes) · about 1.2 s

See
The top strip flies off; light from inside the pack swells up the screen in the best card's colour and slowly settles. One rise, one long fall: at most one flash, and only for a hit. The cards rise out of the pack as a stack, backs up.
Hear
A deep 'whump' (the best card's tier sets its weight) and a rising shimmer.
Move
The pack body drops away; the stack rises 20% and settles.
Reduced flash
The light rises and falls under the flash line; the stack fades up.
Season 0
Season 0's burst: a screen flash at 0.22-0.70 opacity (packBurstPlan: 0.22 + 0.12 a rung) over 620-1300 ms, plus a burst disc and a shock ring, all at once. Measured: counted flashes at the tear in every pack. The new open keeps the size of the moment in sound and motion and spends its light on one slow swell (rise 300 ms, fall 800 ms).

5. Plain cards

a tap or swipe per card · about 3.6 s

See
Each plain card flips with a snap (150 ms) and flies off to the side on the next tap; the next card is under it. A card never flashes white between back and face: the flip turns the card edge-on, so the change is a narrow sliver, never a full-card cut.
Hear
A snap per card, each a step higher in pitch.
Move
The flip turns the card about its long edge in 150 ms; on the next tap it flies off to the right, 300 ms.
Reduced flash
The face cross-fades over 200 ms instead of the flip; no flight (the card slides).
Season 0
Season 0 at an owner's pace (a tap 900 ms after the page takes one) measured no failing second; tapping as fast as the page allows, the card swaps themselves count (a new card face over a field of view is a light change): see the 'fastest' figures. Keeping the flip edge-on and the next card already under the top one keeps a fast thumb under the limit.

6. The build (a held card)

a tap on the face-down shine-slot card · about 2.6 s

See
The room dims (over 500 ms). The card's back glows through its stages (shimmer, charged, radiant, overload: the tell's colours, Season 0's ladder), each stage a rise that holds: the glow only ever climbs, it never drops between stages, so the stages are not flashes. A small pause after each where it might stop.
Hear
The rest of the sound drops near silence; a riser climbs with each stage.
Move
The card trembles, faster each stage (Season 0's tremble).
Reduced flash
No tremble; the glow's stages climb under the flash line; the dim is a 900 ms fade.
Season 0
Season 0's build is already a climb (glow stages, a dim). Kept, with the dim's own fall (the room going darker is a light change too) made slow enough to stay under the line on its own.

7. The hit

time (the build completes) · about 1.8 s

See
The flip (slow for a big pull) and the land: one bloom of light from the card in its finish's colour, rising in 250 ms and falling over 900 ms, while the room comes back up behind it; the faction ward flares and turns; motes in the faction's colour. The finish's own shine (foil sweep, gold sheen) starts after the bloom has fallen.
Hear
A bass hit (its weight by tier), the finish's sting.
Move
A screen shake (Season 0's: up to 12 px for the top tier), the card's settle.
Reduced flash
No shake; the bloom rises and falls under the flash line; the ward fades in without its spin.
Season 0
Season 0's hit: a screen flash at 0.22-1.0 opacity (0.22 + 0.78 x the ladder's weight), 300-800 ms, at the same time as the dim lifting, the ward flaring and the motes: measured as counted flashes at each held card's land. The new hit caps the bloom's height and slows its fall so the bloom and the room coming back can't stack into a second change inside the same second.

8. The summary

a tap after the last card · about 1.5 s

See
The five cards fan in with their finishes and NEW badges; pack points; Open another / Done.
Hear
A soft chord per NEW card.
Move
The fan deals in, 70 ms apart; NEW stamps slam (small).
Reduced flash
The fan fades in together; no slams.
Season 0
Season 0's summary is quiet (kept).

The big pull's takeover (Prototype, Zero-Day), in place of beat 7

7a. Zero-Day: the room goes dark

time (the build completes) · about 0.9 s

See
The room falls to black over 600 ms (slow enough not to count: one change, no return inside the second).
Hear
Everything cuts to silence; a single low tone.
Move
None: the card already lifted by the build hangs still in the dark.
Reduced flash
The same, over 900 ms.
Season 0
Season 0: the screen drops to black with a 'zd on' fade.

7b. Zero-Day: the terminal

time · about 2.2 s

See
A terminal types ZERO-DAY DETECTED, then the breach lines (scanning, privilege ROOT, payload, containment FAILED) in phosphor green. The breach is a glitch: the text tears sideways in bands (motion), not a strobe; the cursor blinks at 1.25 Hz on a tiny area.
Hear
Key clicks per character; a rising alarm, two notes.
Move
Bands of the text slip left and right; no screen shake.
Reduced flash
The text types without the band slips; the cursor holds steady.
Season 0
Season 0: the terminal shakes three times in 1.26 s (0.42 s each) and the Zero-Day shader ramps to full behind it.

7c. Zero-Day: the arrival

time · about 2.0 s

See
The card arrives large and settles; one bloom in the Zero-Day colour (a cold green-white, never saturated red); the ring round the card is a slow turning band in green and violet, not red.
Hear
The rise; the bass hit at its heaviest.
Move
The card scales from 1.5 to 1; a shake as the top tier's.
Reduced flash
No shake, no scale: the card fades in; the bloom under the line; the ring still.
Season 0
Season 0: the arrival, the hit's flash at full weight (1.0 opacity) and a red-green conic ring spinning once every 3 s.
