Deck-size check (owner 2026-10-24's question). Test only: no card, deck, rule or builder change.
Written by engine/src/dev/deckSizeCheck.ts from the files in progress/deck-size/.

== 1. The rule today ==
  Legal size: 20 to 40 cards (MIN_DECK_SIZE, MAX_DECK_SIZE in engine/src/data/decks.ts;
  buildDeck throws outside it, so every deck that plays, built, saved or shipped, passes it).
  The builder imports the same two constants (engine/src/deckbuilder/deckBuilder.ts, MIN_DECK/MAX_DECK).
  The floor was set at stage 3 so the first small test pools (23 and 32 cards) still built; the
  stage-3 note called both numbers a first proposal, not a playtested target.
  Shipped decks: Cortex 40, Swarm 40, Aegis 40, Null 36.
  Other rules that cap size: copy limits (3/3/2/1 by rarity, 1 per Zero-Day card), at most 10 neutral
  cards, removal at most 25% of the deck. An empty deck does not lose: each draw from it deals 2 fatigue.
  How the builder picks a size: the rating start fills to 40 while any usable card is left; the
  designed start fills to the shipped list's own size (Null 36); the search's one-in-five size
  changes grow or shrink by one copy, kept only when it wins clearly more of the same games;
  the confirm step keeps the best of the searched deck and the starts on fresh seeds.
