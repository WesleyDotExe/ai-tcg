checkpoint/report (Cortex-vs-Swarm and Aegis-vs-Cortex are BOTH decisive
[W] 55-60% edges, not "even" — only Null-vs-Cortex and Aegis-vs-Swarm are

     target bands are now DATA in balanceReport.ts itself (RING_DECISIVE /
     RING_EVEN), not hand-recomputed prose after the fact — see the new
     "=== Ring targets ===" section below and the script's own comment on
     why (this exact mislabelling is what hand-computation produces).
     Fixed a real, separate bug found while doing this: the "flagged under
     all three policies" cross-check used to flag a pairing whenever each
     policy independently cleared 60%, WITHOUT checking they agreed on
     WHICH side — see the script's own comment. Swarm-vs-Aegis was
     previously mis-reported as triple-flagged for exactly this reason
     (Aggressive favoured Swarm, Random/Lookahead favoured Aegis — three
     real signals pointing two different directions, not one dominant
     archetype). The cross-check below now requires the same winner.
  2. Cortex-vs-Swarm: NOT touched, per this entry's explicit "not a problem
     to assign a fixer" — still flagged below, as expected.
  3. Aegis-vs-Cortex fix, an AEGIS buff (Cortex was never touched). First
     attempt: a new 0/0-stat Barrier-granting Tool (data/aegisCards.ts,
     "Redundant Barrier Coil") at 1-2 copies — measured WORSE for
     Aegis-vs-Cortex under every policy (the same "zero-immediate-impact
     card crowds out real tempo plays" failure session 11's Aegis
     defensive-card batch already found), so it was NOT shipped. Shipped
     instead: Guard Directive Chip (the pool's own best-performing card by
     played win rate, ~53-56%, a real stat bonus + Guard) bumped 2 -> 3
     copies, its Uncommon rarity ceiling, using the pool's exact remaining
     headroom (39 -> 40 cards, no cut needed).

     destroy reach tightened maxHealth 5 -> 4 (pulls Latent Ward, 5 health,
     out of reach — it sat right on the old boundary); Null Singularity's
     health cut 8 -> 7 (both auras kept — they're its whole point as the
     Mythic capstone; a body cut instead of an aura cut).

Real-play evidence for all three shipped changes:
