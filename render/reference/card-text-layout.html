Rule of thumb: the bottom-left corner holds everything about which series a card belongs to, and the artwork stays clean. A plain number (056/120) means the set, a gold-foil serial above PROTOTYPE · S1 means a Prototype, and PR- means a promo. Only Prototypes use gold foil text.
