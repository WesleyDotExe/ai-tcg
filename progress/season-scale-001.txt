Season scale 001 — recording a match, the Stats page, the standings and the season's end at a season's history
(session 88, game lane)
========================================================================
Machine: 4 cores, Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz; node v22.23.4. Times in ms; median and max of 10 unless "once".
Seven house owners and one player owner (Lattice Warden's, on auto-play), every one in every round, on a file on
disk. History written by recordMatch (one match each a round, 9 minutes apart); then one real round played by the
server's Ladder and each recordPlayed timed (the log and replay files written too). The season end and the next
season's file are made from a copy of the file at each level.
